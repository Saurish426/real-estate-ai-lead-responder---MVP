# Pitch Assets

## Positioning

AI Lead Responder is AI infrastructure for real estate lead conversion.

## One Sentence Startup Description

AI Lead Responder helps real estate agents instantly capture, qualify, respond to, and route inbound leads so fewer inquiries go cold before an agent can follow up.

## 30 Second Pitch

Real estate agents spend time and money generating leads, but too many inquiries go cold because the first response is late, generic, or never happens. AI Lead Responder is AI infrastructure for real estate lead conversion. The MVP captures leads from a website form or manual Gmail test, saves them to a database, extracts buyer, seller, showing, and information intent, generates a guarded professional response, sends an email reply, notifies the agent, tracks conversation memory, and shows activity in a dashboard. When a lead asks for a showing, the product surfaces the booking workflow and can include the agent's calendar link. The goal is simple: help agents respond faster, qualify better, and convert more inbound interest into real conversations.

## 2 Minute Pitch

Real estate lead generation is fragmented. Agents may get inquiries from website forms, listing pages, portals, email, referrals, and paid campaigns. The expensive part is not just generating the lead; it is converting it before the prospect moves on.

Today, many agents still handle that first response manually. If an inquiry comes in while the agent is showing a property, driving, sleeping, or working another deal, the response can be delayed. By the time the agent replies, the lead may have already contacted someone else.

AI Lead Responder is AI infrastructure for real estate lead conversion. The current MVP focuses on the first critical workflow: capture the lead, understand the intent, respond professionally, alert the agent, and make the activity visible.

The product already includes a website lead form, demo lead flow, PostgreSQL lead storage with Prisma, AI extraction for intent and showing interest, AI-generated real estate responses, safety guardrails, email auto-replies through Gmail SMTP, agent notification emails, conversation memory, booking flow support with an agent calendar link, multi-agent foundation, deployment prep, and a dashboard with startup metrics like total leads, AI responses, qualified leads, showing requests, booked leads, response success rate, average response time, and daily or weekly usage.

The wedge is narrow but valuable: make sure every real estate inquiry gets handled immediately and consistently. From there, the product can expand into deeper CRM integrations, more inbox sources, handoff workflows, analytics, and booking automation.

This is not trying to replace agents. It is the infrastructure layer that helps agents respond faster, qualify better, and spend more time with the leads that are ready to move.

## Demo Walkthrough Script

### Opening

"This is AI Lead Responder, AI infrastructure for real estate lead conversion. The product helps agents instantly qualify and respond to inbound leads so fewer inquiries go cold."

### Step 1: Show Homepage

"The homepage is designed as a live demo entry point. It explains the value proposition, shows startup metrics, and gives users a direct path into the lead form, guided demo, and dashboard."

### Step 2: Submit Sample Lead

"I will submit a sample lead asking whether a property is available and requesting a showing this weekend."

### Step 3: AI Extraction

"Once the lead is submitted, the backend saves the lead and runs AI extraction. It identifies the lead's intent, whether they want a showing, their timeline, budget if provided, and a confidence score."

### Step 4: AI Response Generation

"Next, the system generates a short, professional real estate response. Guardrails review the reply so the AI does not confirm a showing, give legal or financial advice, make unsupported availability claims, or create Fair Housing risk."

### Step 5: Email and Agent Notification

"The system sends the customer an email reply and sends the agent a lead summary. If email fails, the lead is still saved and the dashboard still updates."

### Step 6: Booking Flow

"Because this lead asked for a showing, the booking workflow appears. If the agent has saved a calendar link in settings, the response can include that link."

### Step 7: Dashboard Metrics

"The dashboard shows recent leads, AI intent, showing interest, confidence, AI response, email status, booking status, recent events, and startup metrics like total leads, AI responses, qualified leads, showing requests, booked leads, response success rate, average response time, and daily or weekly usage."

### Close

"The current MVP proves the core workflow: capture, qualify, respond, notify, track, and prepare the agent to convert the lead. The next step is turning this into a reliable conversion layer across the places real estate leads already arrive."
