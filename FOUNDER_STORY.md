# Founder Story Draft

## Short Version

I started building AI Lead Responder because real estate agents do not just need more leads; they need a faster and more reliable way to convert the leads they already receive. The first response is often the difference between a live opportunity and a cold inquiry, so I built an MVP that captures a lead, qualifies it with AI, sends a professional response, alerts the agent, and tracks the result in a dashboard.

## Longer Draft

Real estate is a relationship business, but the first few minutes after a lead arrives are increasingly technical. A buyer may ask for a showing from a website form. A seller may email about listing their home. A prospect may ask a basic property question outside normal business hours. In each case, the agent needs to respond quickly, professionally, and safely.

The problem is that agents are often busy doing the work that makes them valuable: meeting clients, showing properties, preparing offers, negotiating, and managing active deals. When a new lead arrives during that time, the response can be delayed or inconsistent. That delay can turn paid lead generation into missed opportunity.

AI Lead Responder began as a focused MVP around that exact moment. The product captures a lead, saves it, extracts intent with AI, generates a guarded response, sends an email reply, notifies the agent, stores conversation memory, supports showing workflows, and displays results in a dashboard.

The goal is not to replace the agent. The goal is to give agents an infrastructure layer that makes sure every inbound inquiry gets an immediate first touch, clear qualification, and a clean handoff.

The current product is still an MVP, but it proves the core workflow end to end. The next challenge is making it production-grade, connecting more lead sources, and turning the dashboard into a daily operating system for real estate lead conversion.
